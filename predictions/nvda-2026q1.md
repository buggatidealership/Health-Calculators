# NVIDIA Q1 FY2027 Earnings Prediction

**Reporting:** May 20, 2026, after market close
**Author:** Claude Opus 4.7 (Claude Code session)
**Made before the print** — unhedged, single-direction call

---

## The call, one line

**BEAT across the board.** Revenue beats consensus and clears the buy-side whisper. Q2 guide beats. Gross margin holds the line at ~75%. The print is a clean beat-and-raise — the open question is only whether it clears the *euphoric* bar, not whether it clears the published one.

## Thesis (why beat, not miss)

**1. The setup is sandbagged.** Q1 guide of $78B was given Feb 25 with explicit "no China DC compute" assumption. NVIDIA has beaten its own revenue guide every quarter of FY26 — Q4 was $68.1B against a ~$65B guide, a 4.6% beat over the midpoint. A repeat of that magnitude alone lands at ~$81.5B. I'm slightly inside that because Blackwell ramp is no longer the kicker (already ~70% of DC last quarter — the easy comp uplift is in the base).

**2. Hyperscaler capex re-rated UP, hard, after the Q1 reporting season.** Aggregate 2026 capex from MSFT/GOOG/META/AMZN is now ~$725B vs $410B in 2025 (+77%). Numbers from the actual prints:
   - Amazon $44.2B Q1 capex, $200B FY26 guide
   - Alphabet $35.7B Q1, GCP backlog >$460B, $175–185B FY26
   - Microsoft $30.9B fiscal Q3, AI revenue run-rate >$37B
   - Meta raised FY26 to $125–145B from $115–135B
   These are *NVIDIA's checks coming in*. The April quarter is the first one fully reflecting that re-rate at the supplier.

**3. Supply chain reads confirm the demand.** TSMC Q1 HPC was +20% q/q at 61% of revenue; Q2 guide $39–40.2B is up sharply. SK Hynix posted record Q1 with HBM-led memory still in undersupply. HBM3E pricing up ~20% into 2026. None of that supports a NVDA revenue miss; it supports volume + ASP both contributing.

**4. AMD is a tell, not a threat at this scale.** AMD Q1 DC revenue was $5.8B (+57% YoY), MI350 ramping, $11.2B Q2 guide. AMD is real but it's roughly 8% the size of NVIDIA's DC business. AMD's beat means *the pie grew*, not that NVIDIA lost share — both AMD's MI350 and Meta's announced 6GW of AMD GPUs are *incremental* to NVDA's own bookings, not substitutionary in the April quarter.

**5. Sell-side is openly chasing.** Morgan Stanley (May 18) raised PT to $285 and bumped Apr Q to $79.26B / Jul Q to $87.88B. Goldman is at $80.05B on revenue, calendar-year EPS 14–34% above Street, PT $250. KeyBanc $300. When the sell-side is pre-walking estimates *into* the print, they're catching up to data they already know.

**6. China is upside, not downside, vs the guide.** The H20 ban easing in May 2026 and the H200 approvals (Alibaba/Tencent/ByteDance, 75K-unit caps) are *not* in the $78B guide. Even if deliveries are slow ("limited" per recent reporting), any in-quarter China contribution lands as pure beat. The bear case here is that this is mostly an FY27 H2 story, but the optionality is asymmetric to the upside on this print.

**7. Margins hold.** Q4 FY26 was 75.2% non-GAAP. Q1 guide 75% ± 50bps. The Blackwell ramp's worst margin compression was the prior 2 quarters; first Rubin samples shipped (Kress confirmed) means *initial* Rubin cost burden is small and Blackwell mix is now mature. I have 75.2% as base case, with a real chance of 75.5%+ given pricing power signals (HBM cost is passed through, system ASPs on GB300 NVL72 are higher).

---

## What I'm explicitly betting against

- **The "beat-and-fade" trade.** With NVDA at $221.75 and a 12.9% options-implied move (~$28), the published consensus is *not* what matters — the buy-side whisper at $80B+ revenue and $90B Q2 guide is. I am calling that NVIDIA *clears the whisper on guide*. If they only clear the published consensus and land Q2 at $86B, that's a "technical beat, real miss" and the stock fades. I'm taking the harder side: guide clears whisper.
- **A China negative surprise.** Possible they take another inventory charge or warn on geopolitical risk. I don't think so — the recent policy direction is permissive and Jensen joined Trump's May China visit.
- **A margin disappointment.** Possible if Rubin sampling costs flowed through faster than expected. Low probability given Kress's commentary and the ramp profile.

---

## Confidence

- Revenue beat vs published consensus: **~90%**
- Revenue beat vs buy-side whisper ($80B+): **~55%**
- Q2 guide above published consensus: **~85%**
- Q2 guide above whisper (~$90B): **~50%**  ← the swing factor
- Gross margin ≥75% non-GAAP: **~70%**
- EPS beat vs consensus: **~85%**

Composite: this is a beat. The interesting question is the *magnitude vs the whisper*, and I'm calling NVIDIA clears it — but barely. Single-line headline: **clean beat, raise above consensus, slightly under the most euphoric whispers, margins hold.**
